What kind of strip is it?

Apply this and watch the strip: it shows red, green, blue and white one after the other. Pick another colour order until the colours match.

How many LEDs, and where is the middle?

Apply and watch the strip: the first pixel is red, the middle one green and the last one blue. The last lit pixel is your LED count.
